One funny line, 200 characters or fewer, for the [make.fun bounty "Make the Judge Laugh in One Line"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-email-baru-make-the-judge-laugh-in-one-line-791ccb21).

## The entry

> I told my AI therapist I feel invisible. It replied: “Sorry — who is this?”

## Why it works

The setup promises empathy; the punchline delivers the opposite — the therapist itself proves the speaker's point. The joke lands on the final three words, with nothing left to explain.

## How it runs

`main.py` validates the line against the bounty gate (UTF-8, 1–200 characters, a single line, not only whitespace) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
